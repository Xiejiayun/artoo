Artoo support

Get connected to your team, recover access, or report an iOS client issue.

Connect your phone

  1. Ask your administrator for your team's HTTPS Artoo server address and account access.
  2. Sign in to the team's web app using your own account. Create an iOS pairing code.
  3. Open Artoo on your phone, enter the server address and your code, then pair.

A pairing code carries the creating account's permissions. Never exchange codes with another member or post them in support issues.

Connection or account problems

Confirm the server is reachable from your phone and that your account has access. An expired or revoked session requires a fresh pairing code. If sign-out could not reach the server, ask an administrator to revoke the phone from Devices.

The iOS client uses your team's service. Contact your team administrator for account access, server outages, billing for separately provided services, data deletion and retention requests.

Agent work is waiting

Check that the selected execution computer is connected and the requested task has its required approval. The phone does not run the agent process itself. Keep the task and run identifiers when asking your administrator about failed or stopped work.

Report a client problem

Open an Artoo support issue with the app version, iOS version, device model and a short description of the steps and expected behavior. The publisher is Jiayun Xie.

Issues are public.

Do not attach pairing codes, credentials, private team messages, project files or raw XCTest/diagnostic bundles. Remove private information from any screenshot before posting. For sensitive account or workspace matters, contact your team operator privately instead.
