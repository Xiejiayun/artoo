Artoo iOS privacy policy

Artoo connects to the team server you choose. This policy explains the iOS client's handling of information and how to obtain help.

Publisher and scope

Jiayun Xie develops and publishes the Artoo iOS client. The client does not include a publisher-operated team hosting service. Your team's server operator controls the workspace, accounts, access, server storage, execution computers and configured AI providers. Ask that operator for the policy and contact information applicable to its service before connecting.

Information the client handles

  • Connection and identity: your server address, a server-issued device identifier and the sign-in credential. Pairing sends the one-time code, device name, platform and app version to the selected server. Authenticated requests identify your account and device.
  • Workspace information: account names, email addresses and roles returned by your server; conversations, mentions, task and goal details, approvals, execution history, audit records and files you access or submit.
  • Drafts and files: unsent messages and pending sends, downloaded previews and exported audit records stored locally on the phone.

This information supports the requested workspace features. The client does not include advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs, and does not send automatic analytics to the publisher. Your server operator and its providers may maintain their own logs.

Where information goes

The client sends workspace requests and content to the server address you supply. Other authorized team members may access content according to that server's permissions. When you request an agent response or start work, the team's execution computer may send conversation/task context and relevant workspace files to its configured AI provider. The provider, processing location and retention terms depend on your team's setup. Check these with your administrator before sending sensitive content.

Opening the privacy/support pages contacts their hosting provider, GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information under its privacy statement. Sharing an artifact gives a copy to the app or destination you select, whose handling is outside Artoo's control.

Local storage and security

Connection credentials are held in the device's Keychain, accessible while unlocked, and are not synchronized through iCloud Keychain. The client keeps only its control credential, not the execution computer's credential. Drafts and pending sends use local app settings scoped to server, account and conversation. Preview and audit-export files use iOS file protection. Use HTTPS for a shared team server.

Retention, sign-out and deletion

Signing out clears the local sign-in credential and asks the server to revoke that session. If the server cannot be reached, revocation is not confirmed; your administrator can revoke the device from the team workspace. Sign-out does not delete drafts, temporary files, copies you shared, or records retained by servers and AI providers.

Your team operator is the contact for account or workspace-content deletion, retention periods, backups and downstream provider requests. The publisher cannot access or delete records on independently operated team servers. Removing the app through iOS removes its app data container; sign out first to clear the active connection credential. Copies you shared elsewhere must be managed in their destinations.

Support information you choose to share

If you open a GitHub support issue, the issue and information you submit are public under GitHub's policies. Submit only a minimal, non-sensitive description. Do not post pairing codes, credentials, private workspace content or raw diagnostic bundles. Public issues can remain available in repository history and platform copies; contact the repository maintainer through the issue for correction or removal requests that concern the publisher's repository.

Questions and changes

For client questions, use the support page. For private account/data requests, contact your own server operator directly. We will update this page when the client's data handling changes and update the effective date. A team operator's policy may change separately.
